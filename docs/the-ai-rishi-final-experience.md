# THE AI RISHI — final experience

This is the experience the site is actually shipping on `feature/immersive-visual-upgrade`. It is not a collage of other sites.

## Product thesis

THE AI RISHI is a learning environment. The current programme is FORGE-120. The visitor should feel they have opened that programme, not a marketing page about it.

Brand, exact:

- THE AI RISHI
- ANCIENT WISDOM · MODERN INTELLIGENCE

The programme is not an AI course. Foundations come first. Retrieval and controlled tool use are days 97–105. Day 105 is awareness, not a specialisation claim. Day 120 is a checkpoint, not mastery.

## Emotional journey

Open: a warm graphite desk and an ivory sheet. The sheet is the current phase, drawn as structure, not as an ornament.

Move: the ten real phases are a rail. Choosing one moves the page to that phase. Scrolling does the same. The drawing, the day list, and the header readout change together.

Continue: below the desk, the same catalogue becomes the day map, then the reliability and control instruments, then the configured sections (method, why, path, destinations, call to action).

Leave: a lesson is a focused page in the same bar, not a different product.

## OS-like model, without copying an OS

There is no dock, no traffic lights, no window chrome, no sci-fi HUD.

The original model is a desk:

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| System bar | Brand, current phase, navigation, search, Start Day 1 |
| Phase rail | The ten stages. This is how you move. |
| Ivory sheet | The phase, drawn |
| Day panel | That phase’s real days. Published days open. Planned days stay titles. |
| Colophon | Brand, tagline, programme sentence, the two real calls to action |
| Instruments | Days 89–105, interactive, below the desk |

Direct URLs, back, and keyboard focus stay. Phase choice does not rewrite history. It scrolls.

## Homepage composition

First viewport, still, with motion off:

- Rail of phase names
- Ivory sheet showing Foundations: one column, one beam
- Panel: Foundations, days 1–12, the real day titles
- Colophon: THE AI RISHI, the exact tagline, FORGE-120, the programme sentence

Scroll or a rail button replaces the sheet. Kubernetes is a cluster. Reliability is a signal. Retrieval is documents and a citation. Design and defence is those structures inside one closed frame.

## Curriculum visualization

Phase names and day ranges come from the catalogue, which comes from the FORGE-120 snapshot. The site does not invent a second set of labels.

## AI / RAG visualization

Distinct from the infrastructure drawings. On the sheet, retrieval is documents, one selected chunk, and a line back. Below, the instruments are HTML the visitor operates:

- Days 89–96: the figure changes with the real day
- Chunk choice: answer, miss, or block
- Review-only loop: a shell is blocked, a read-only function is not
- Injection and golden eval: pass and fail are states, labelled as fixtures
- MCP boundary and the nine gates

No live model. No live index. No fake counts.

## Future media and configuration

Homepage order and visibility are `platform.json` `homepage.sections`. Each section has `enabled` and `order`.

Turning a section off removes it. No redesign.

Already in that list, off until there is real material: topics, recent writing, guides list, projects list, extra courses. Destinations render only real social links. Search already covers days, guides, and projects. YouTube and Instagram carousels are not fabricated. A channel or content-list section can be enabled when the files exist.

Adding a programme later is another record in `programs.json`, not a new homepage.

## Mobile

Below 960px the rail is a horizontal strip of phase numbers. The active name stays readable. The day list is behind a toggle so the sheet remains visible. The colophon stacks. Touch targets stay at least 44px where they are actions.

## Accessibility

The sheet is decorative. The rail, day list, instruments, and colophon are text. Reduced motion keeps the same drawing and uses an instant scroll. Focus rings stay. The header readout is hidden from assistive tech because the rail and panel say the same thing.

## Performance

No WebGL on the homepage. The drawing is one SVG. Phase changes are discrete. The frame does not allocate a scene graph. Search stays a dialog opened on demand.

## Design principles

- A mark means a phase, a path, a boundary, a signal, or a source.
- Motion changes which of those you are looking at.
- Copy comes from the catalogue, the platform file, or the brand file.
- The brand name is not a prefix for components.

## Anti-patterns

Lotus, rings, particle fields, purple wash, neural wallpaper, sci-fi HUD, fake live users, fake mastery scores, invented taglines, “Rishi OS”, a 3D object beside a normal landing page, a 1000-row research sheet of unread duplicates.

## Benchmark, without padding

A previous research file was rejected because rows repeated a template. This pass does not add another thousand cloned entries.

Patterns actually used, from work that is specific enough to name:

- A field that is the navigation, not a hero above it (the phase rail is the way you move).
- One object that changes job instead of a new illustration per section (the same sheet).
- A side index of the real content, not a caption (the day panel).
- Instruments you operate, not diagrams that play themselves (the control section).
- A still frame that is already the layout (ivory sheet, rail, colophon).

Studios and sites were looked at for those patterns — Active Theory’s persistent worlds, Lusion’s object-as-interface, editorial indexes that stay usable, technical diagrams that teach a system. None of them are copied. The desk, the ten drawings, and the FORGE-120 names are this product.
