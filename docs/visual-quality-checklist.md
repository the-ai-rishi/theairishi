Checked against the rendered paper field, not the build log.

## Findings

- Home is a plate. The phase name sits on the construction. The construction changes with the phase: staff, boundary, path, strata, nodes, managed band, return path, instrument, citation, closed evidence frame.
- The day ruler uses the real day numbers for that phase. It is not ten equal boxes.
- The span below the field sizes each phase by its day count.
- The old desk, constellation, halo, footer seed, and `universe.css` are not in the build.
- The slogan is not visible. The lockup is THE AI RISHI. The mark is the eight-petal logo.
- Learn is day tiles. Phone learn is one phase per screen.
- Guides is a poster. Labs are posters plus the lab’s recorded technologies.
- About is still chapters of configured type. It is the weakest surface.
- Search is a full-screen command layer.
- The instrument section is paper, not a dark panel. Fixtures are labelled. No live model.
- Phone: previous/next, a plate tall enough to read, day rows, and every configured nav item.
- Reduced motion still shows the phase, the days, and the actions.
- Disabled homepage sections stay off. No fake YouTube or Instagram.
